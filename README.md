# Smoove Prep

Meal planning for Smoove Cafe, Melksham: recipes, nutrition, the 14 UK allergens, costing, shopping lists and food labels.

## Using it
Open `index.html` in a browser. No install or account is needed. To host it online for free, turn on GitHub Pages for this repo (Settings → Pages → deploy from branch).

- **Meal plan**: build a week (Breakfast, Lunch, Snack, Dinner) and set how many customers it's for. Each day shows calories, protein, carbs, fat and food cost against your targets, and the allergens in the week.
- **Recipes**: nutrition, allergens, cost per portion and GP % (after VAT) are worked out from the ingredients.
- **Ingredients**: nutrition per 100g, £ per kg and allergens. Use your suppliers' spec sheets.
- **Shopping list**: totals for the whole plan, multiplied by customer numbers, with an estimated cost.
- **Labels**: printable labels with ingredients listed by weight, allergens in bold, nutrition per 100g and per portion, and a use-by date.
- **Settings**: business details, VAT, GP target, shelf life, daily targets, and backup export/import.

Data is saved in the browser you use. Export a backup from Settings regularly.

The starter nutrition and price figures are examples only. Check them before you rely on a label.
